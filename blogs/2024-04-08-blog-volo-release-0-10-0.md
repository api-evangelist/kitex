In Volo 0.10.0, we mainly focused on extensibility and ease of use. Break Change Error Handling Refactored Existing error types had issues such as unclear semantics, lack of maintainability and extensibility, and misuse. In the new version, we refactored the entire error handling, greatly enhancing the clarity and maintainability of error handling, and reducing misuse through the type system.
