Volo is a lightweight, high-performance, scalable, and user-friendly Rust RPC framework developed by the ByteDance service framework team. It leverages the latest GAT and TAIT features of Rust to deliver exceptional performance and efficiency. Within ByteDance, Volo has been extensively used, implementing multiple business and foundational components.
