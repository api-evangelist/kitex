Background On September 8, 2021, ByteDance officially announced the open-sourcing of CloudWeGo . CloudWeGo is a set of microservices middleware developed by ByteDance, characterized by high performance, strong scalability, and high stability. It focuses on solving the difficulties of microservices communication and governance, and meets the demands of different businesses in different scenarios.
